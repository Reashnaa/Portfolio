Campus Printing Analysis.

Monthly Usage & Printer Distribution — exploring how printing activity varies across Republic Polytechnic schools and printers.

Data period: January–June 20255,140 print jobsTool: Tableau

Project overview

As part of my Data Analytics coursework at Republic Polytechnic, I used a provided campus printing dataset to create a Tableau dashboard. The goal was to explore how printing activity differed between schools and how individual printers were used over six months.

I worked with related data tables in Tableau using logical relationships rather than physical joins, then created visualisations to compare trends and identify patterns.

Business questions

  • How does printing activity vary across schools from month to month?
  • How is printing demand distributed across printers, and how does their usage change over time?

Key findings & analysis

01 / Printing demand across schools

SEG recorded the highest printing activity with 1,514 print jobs (29.5%), while SSH had the lowest with 617 (12.0%). SEG also led in every month shown. This suggests that printing demand differs across schools, although student population sizes would need to be considered before comparing printing behaviour per student.

02 / Printer usage over time

PR03 handled 1,215 jobs (23.6%) across the full period, the highest of any printer. It also had the largest share each month, ranging from 21.8% in January to 25.7% in June. PR09 had the lowest overall usage at 142 jobs (2.8%), with monthly shares ranging from 1.8% to 4.1%.

These differences suggest that some printers consistently receive more print jobs than others. The data does not establish whether this is due to location, convenience, capacity or printer availability.

Possible next steps

RP could review printer locations, availability and maintenance needs to better understand why demand is uneven. Comparing print jobs per student would also help make school-level comparisons more meaningful.

How I used AI

I used AI as a support tool while working on this Tableau coursework, including seeking guidance and clarifying parts of the analysis and visualisation process. I used that assistance alongside my own work in Tableau to develop and review the dashboard.

What I learned

This coursework helped me practise using Tableau Relationships, building dashboards, comparing trends over time and explaining what the data might mean for operational decisions.
